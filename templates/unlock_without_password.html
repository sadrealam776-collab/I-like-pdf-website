Frequently Asked Questions

How does unlocking a PDF without password work?

Most restricted PDFs use permissions/owner locks that block copying or printing. LikePDF bypasses these restriction layers and extracts clean pages into an unrestricted PDF.

Can it remove open passwords if the file won't open at all?

If a PDF has heavy user/open encryption (AES-256) where you cannot view pages, our system attempts blank/default keys. If strongly encrypted, the opening password is required via our standard Unlock PDF tool.

Are my uploaded PDF files kept private?

Yes. All uploads are processed over encrypted HTTPS channels and automatically purged from our servers within 60 minutes with zero copies or backups retained.
