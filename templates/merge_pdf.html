How it works:

  1. Click "Select Files" and choose the PDFs you want to combine.
  2. Arrange them in the order you want the final document to follow.
  3. Click "Merge PDF."
  4. Download your combined file — it's ready in seconds.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a limit on how many PDFs I can merge?

No. You can merge as many files as you need in a single session.

Will merging affect the quality of my PDF?

No. Merging only combines pages — it doesn't re-compress or alter the content, so quality stays exactly as it was.

Do I need to sign up to use this?

No account or email is required. Upload, merge, download.

What happens to my files after I download the merged PDF?

They're automatically deleted from our servers within 60 minutes. We don't keep copies.

Can I merge PDFs on my phone?

Yes — the tool works in any mobile browser, no app download needed.
