How it works:

  1. Upload the PDF you want to split.
  2. Choose the pages you want to extract, or enter page ranges.
  3. Click "Split PDF."
  4. Download your new file(s).

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I extract just one page from a PDF?

Yes — select the single page you want and it will be saved as its own PDF.

Will splitting reduce the quality of my document?

No. The pages keep their original resolution and formatting.

Is my file kept after splitting?

No — it's deleted automatically within 60 minutes of upload.

Do I need to install anything?

No. This runs entirely in your browser.
