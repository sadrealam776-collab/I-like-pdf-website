Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How do I convert WEBP images back to standard JPG or PNG format?

Upload your downloaded .webp file, select JPG or PNG as the target format from the dropdown menu, and click Convert. The output file will download instantly ready for any viewer or editor.

Will converting a PNG image to JPG keep its transparent background?

The JPG format does not support transparency. When converting transparent PNGs to JPG, LikePDF automatically composites the background onto clean solid white. If you need transparency, convert to PNG or WEBP instead.

Is there any quality loss during format conversion?

LikePDF uses 95% maximum quality encoding and full RGB/RGBA color space preservation to ensure your converted images look identical to the original.

Are my uploaded photos safe and private?

Yes. All uploads are processed over encrypted HTTPS connections and completely purged from our servers within 60 minutes with zero data backups retained.
