Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to know the current password to unlock the file?

Yes — this tool removes a password you already know; it isn't a password-cracking tool.

Is this legal to use?

Yes, for files you own or have permission to access. Please don't use this on documents you don't have rights to.

Will unlocking affect the content of my PDF?

No — only the password protection is removed; the content stays the same.
