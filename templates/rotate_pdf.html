Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Will rotating a PDF change its page orientation permanently?

Yes! When you rotate and save your PDF with LikePDF, the new rotation angle (90°, 180°, or 270°) is saved directly to the PDF metadata, so it opens right-side up on any device or printer.

Can I rotate a scanned landscape document back to portrait?

Yes! Select 90° Clockwise or Counter-Clockwise to instantly flip sideways scanned documents back into vertical portrait reading mode.

Does rotating a PDF cause loss of text quality or resolution?

Not at all. LikePDF modifies the internal matrix coordinate rotation rather than rasterizing pages, keeping all vector text and images razor-sharp.

Is my uploaded PDF kept private?

Yes. All uploaded files are transferred via TLS-encrypted connections and automatically deleted permanently from our servers after 60 minutes.
