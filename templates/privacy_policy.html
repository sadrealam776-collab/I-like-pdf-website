Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Core Commitment & Zero-Log Policy

At LikePDF (https://likepdf.in/), your personal privacy and file integrity are our top priorities. We believe online utilities should operate transparently without inspecting, collecting, or retaining your personal documents, images, or metadata.

2. The 60-Minute Automated File Purge Policy

When you upload a document or image to any of our conversion or editing tools:

  • Files are processed either purely in high-speed RAM buffers or in isolated temporary directories.
  • All original files and processed output files are automatically and permanently deleted within 60 minutes of execution.
  • We do not create backups, mirror copies, or permanent archives of your files.

3. SSL/TLS End-to-End Encryption

All file transfers to and from our servers are encrypted using modern Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS). This ensures your documents cannot be intercepted, read, or modified by third parties during transmission.

4. No User Registration Required

You can use every tool on LikePDF without creating an account, linking social profiles, or providing an email address. We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal information.

5. Contacting Us

If you have questions or inquiries regarding our privacy standards, contact us via our Contact Page.
