Privacy Policy

At LikePDF, accessible from https://likepdf.in, your privacy is our highest priority. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information collected and recorded by LikePDF and how we use it.

Automatic File Deletion

All documents, PDFs, and images uploaded to our platform for editing, conversion, or compression are handled over encrypted channels and automatically deleted permanently within 60 minutes.

Log Files

LikePDF follows a standard procedure of using log files. These files log visitors when they visit websites. The information collected includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamp, referring/exit pages, and possibly the number of clicks.
