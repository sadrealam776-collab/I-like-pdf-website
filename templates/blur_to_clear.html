Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How does the AI unblur images without making them noisy?

LikePDF uses adaptive contrast enhancement (CLAHE) combined with bilateral edge-preserving filters. This boosts contrast along soft edges and text outlines while suppressing speckle background noise.

Can this tool make blurry text on scanned documents readable?

Yes! The adaptive contrast filter specifically highlights soft text edges, making out-of-focus receipts, scanned notes, and document photos significantly sharper and easier to read.

Are my uploaded photos stored on your server?

No. Images are processed directly in high-speed server memory (RAM buffer) and never stored on hard disk, ensuring 100% privacy and zero data retention.

Is there a limit on how many images I can sharpen?

No. You can enhance as many images as you need completely free, without any daily limits, accounts, or watermarks.
